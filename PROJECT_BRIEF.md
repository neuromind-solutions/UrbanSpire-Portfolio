# Project Brief — Urbanspire Group

## Purpose
Create a premium corporate website for Urbanspire Group, a group consisting of three companies. The website should explain the group structure, present services and approved projects, and provide a clear route for visitors to contact the appropriate team.

## Experience Goals
- Simple, professional, clean, modern, and impressive.
- High-quality cinematic hero video on the homepage.
- Clear presentation of the parent group and its three companies.
- Strong project imagery and editorial layouts.
- Easy content maintenance.
- Responsive, accessible, fast, secure, and search-friendly.

## Audience
The final audience segments and geographic focus must be confirmed with the client. Do not assume target markets, sectors, or customer profiles without evidence.

## Proposed Content Areas
- Home
- About the Group
- Our Companies
- Services
- Projects and Project Details
- Contact

Careers, News, Articles, or other sections may be added when approved and when the content/workflow is ready. Hide empty editorial navigation rather than implying content exists.

## Group Structure
The site must show three company profiles under Urbanspire Group. Until approved details are supplied, use clearly marked placeholders such as `Company One (placeholder)`. Do not invent legal names, service lines, locations, history, leadership, or credentials.

## Content and Brand Inputs Needed
- Approved legal/public group name and company names
- Approved company descriptions and service lists
- Approved logo and brand assets
- Approved project list and project facts
- Licensed photography and hero video
- Approved public contact details and domain
- Any approved certifications, awards, client names, and performance claims
- Privacy/legal copy appropriate to the final implementation

## Scope Discipline
Start with the public portfolio experience. Add CMS management and other workflows in separate reviewed phases. The project is at the planning/specification stage until implementation is verified.
