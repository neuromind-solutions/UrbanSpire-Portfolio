# Content Guidelines — Urbanspire Group

## Accuracy First
Never fabricate:
- Legal or trading names
- Company history, locations, leadership, or service claims
- Projects, project status, completion dates, or project specifications
- Clients, partners, testimonials, awards, certifications, or accreditations
- Statistics, project values, quantities, or performance results
- Phone numbers, email addresses, or physical addresses

If a fact is missing, mark it as awaiting approval or ask for the correct information.

## Demo Content
Temporary copy and fictional examples may be used during prototyping only when clearly marked as demo content. Make demo content easy to find and replace. Do not let placeholder claims reach production.

## Stock Images and Video
- Verify the license and permitted use before adding media.
- Track source and license information where practical.
- Do not present stock imagery as evidence of Urbanspire's work.
- Avoid unlicensed logos, identifiable client branding, or misleading project captions.
- Treat the hero video as illustrative unless the client confirms it shows a real company project.

## Tone
Use clear, concise, professional language. Avoid unsupported superlatives and vague claims such as “industry-leading” unless approved and substantiated. Prioritize specific, verified information.

## Publishing
Production content must be reviewed by an authorized company representative. Confirm spelling, names, dates, project permissions, image rights, links, and contact details before publication.
